Step-by-Step Digital Tax Filing Guide

A practical India-focused guide. Always check the official portal for the latest rules, forms, deadlines and eligibility.

01

Prepare Your Documents

Keep your PAN, Aadhaar, bank details, sales and purchase records, invoices, expense records, TDS details, previous return and other applicable tax documents ready.

02

Check Your Tax Requirement

Identify whether you need to file an Income Tax Return, GST return, TDS statement, or another applicable form. Requirements depend on your taxpayer/business situation.

03

Use the Official Income Tax Portal

For income-tax returns and related services, use the official Income Tax Department e-Filing portal. Do not use unofficial websites for filing or entering sensitive information.

Open Income Tax e-Filing ↗
04

Register or Log In

If you are a new taxpayer, register using an active PAN and the required contact details. Existing users can log in to access e-Filing services.

05

Select the Correct Return/Form

Choose the applicable ITR or tax form for your taxpayer category and assessment year. Read the eligibility conditions before proceeding.

06

Enter and Verify Information

Enter income, deductions, taxes paid, bank information and other required details. Compare the information with your records and available tax statements before submitting.

07

Check Your Tax Calculation

Review the calculated tax, refund or amount payable. If tax is payable, follow the portal's applicable payment process before completing the return.

08

Submit the Return

Preview the return, check all declarations and submit it through the applicable online filing process. Follow the portal instructions for verification.

09

Complete e-Verification

Complete the applicable e-Verification process after filing. Keep the acknowledgement/ITR-V and other filing records safely for future reference.

10

Track Your Filing

Use the official e-Filing portal to check return status, notices, refunds, filed forms and other available services. Keep digital backups of important records.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for first-time digital filers.

Is digital tax filing difficult?

It can seem difficult initially, but understanding the workflow and keeping records organized makes the process easier.

What should I do if I make a mistake?

Do not ignore it. Check the applicable correction or amendment procedure and seek professional advice when required.

Is it safe to file taxes online?

Use only official government portals, secure devices and trusted internet connections. Never share passwords, PINs or OTPs.

Do all small businesses have the same tax requirements?

No. Requirements can depend on business type, turnover, registration status, transactions and other factors.
